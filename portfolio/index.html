AlgoLearn India

A platform for learning algorithmic trading on Indian markets. You describe a strategy in plain English and get Python code, backtest it on live NSE/BSE data, paper-trade past charts, and prove every result on a Hyperledger Besu blockchain.

  • Plain English → Python strategies (Gemini, with a rule-based fallback)
  • Sandboxed backtester with Indian charges, slippage & look-ahead-bias checks
  • Server-authoritative simulator, ledger explorer & proof bundles
